03 / Hotel SEO

Boutique hotel SEO: Tahoe Mountain Inn

A family-run boutique property in South Lake Tahoe returned for a second, larger project, with a site where the rooms, the thing they actually sell, were invisible in search.

Explore the story

The obvious reading of their data was wrong. The 37 room pages showed 26,787 appearances in Google over 90 days, more than the homepage. The queries behind them showed 21,012 of those were sitelinks under brand searches, not room demand. Strip the brand out and the room pages had 244 appearances and one click in three months.

The real ceiling was duplication: of 528 room-page pairs, 298 shared more than 80% of their wording, and the median pair was word-for-word identical. Rather than invent differences, I asked the owner for a room attribute record and rewrote all 33 rooms from real features. No pair now shares more than 22%.

Alongside that: homepage mobile load time went from 13.6s to 4.7s, layout shift to zero, 44 structured data entities built and validated, 119 missing alt tags fixed, five landing pages built, FAQ sections written from real search queries, and one address across the site, schema and Google Business Profile.

Four searches the property had never ranked for now sit on page one. None of the 18 tracked searches were lost. I also found 23 rooms advertising a fireplace they do not have and a dog policy printed backwards on 40 pages. Neither was in the brief.

Result. 43 pages, September 2026: mobile load time 13.6s to 4.7s, 44 structured data entities, four new page-one positions and no tracked searches lost.

06 / Full SEO

Full SEO for PJ Bold

PJ Bold crafts unique, high-quality molds. Their products were sought after, but the website wasn’t bringing in new customers organically.

Explore the story

Over a 6-month baseline, they had 3.57K clicks, 324K impressions, a 1.1% CTR and an average search position of 25.9.

We ran a full site audit, did targeted keyword research around mold-making queries, optimized existing content, improved the site architecture, built quality backlinks, rewrote product descriptions for SEO, and created over 50 blog posts to establish them as a thought leader in the space.

Result. Over the next 6 months, clicks increased 29.89% to 4.66K, impressions grew 46.30% to 475K, and the average position improved from 25.9 to 19.9. The site started ranking for a much wider range of commercial and informational terms in the mold-making niche.
